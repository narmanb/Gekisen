[b]Dragonball Ruination – Android version[/b]
Everything added and fixed in this version of the game, newest first. The original game's own notes are under Legacy Update Notes.

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/8/2026 – New numbers, stat points, rebirth[/b][/color]
- Numbers are much closer to the series: Humans start around 50 powerlevel, Saiyans 150, Changelings 1,000 (genes add or take up to 20%). Reaching a billion takes a long time
- Monsters are on the same scale and keep their order: bunnies around 8, Saibamen around 120, Saiyan Warriors around 1,500, Super Saiyan mobs around 2.5 million, the strongest God-tier monsters and Beerus' planet in the billions
- Damage scales with any numbers: attack x attack / (attack + defence). Weaker fighters still do a little, but no more hitting for exactly 1 forever
- Levels: every level takes a bit more exp than the last; past level 1,000 levels come much slower and give much less. Technique, transformation, teacher and rebirth levels moved to the new scale (battle techniques at level 40)
- Training gains grow with your stats (Self Train raises the focused stat by more as you get stronger); multipliers speed it up
- Mobbing is the fastest way to get stronger: every kill raises your Strength, Defence and Ki, more for tougher monsters, and gives more exp; weak monsters give next to nothing
- Exploding Saibamen and Cells give a tenth of a level per kill instead of a whole one
- Transformations: Form 1 x2 (Super Saiyan), Form 2 x3, Form 3 x4, Form 4 x6, then x8, x10, x12; step races x1.5 per form, stacking and always on
- Saga bosses use series powerlevels: Raditz 1,500, Nappa 4,000, Vegeta 18,000, Ginyu 120,000, Frieza 530,000 to 1.8 million (scaled by difficulty)
- Stat Points (from the original): when you create a character you get 5 points for the Powerlevel, Ki, Strength and Defense multipliers (up to 10 each) or Stamina
- Rebirth (from the original): talk to the Omni King in the Other World at level 1,000 (+200 per rebirth, up to 2,000). Start over at level 1 as any race and gene, keeping your name and zenni, with +1 stat point and +1 multiplier cap per rebirth, more exp (up to x3) and +10% training and mobbing gains per rebirth
- Old saves can't carry over to the new numbers; they're kept as a backup and you start a new character

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/8/2026 – Dodgeable homing[/b][/color]
- Saga bosses' ki volleys fly at the spot you were standing on; stronger bosses' shots re-aim at you once or twice on the way, so moving still gets you out of the way
- Your Dodge skill now also lets you dodge homing shots (as well as melee); straight blasts and beams can't be dodged

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/8/2026 – Sagas trimmed, Double Sunday sprite[/b][/color]
- Sagas now go up to Frieza: the Villain Saga is Raditz, Vegeta and Nappa, the Ginyu Force and Frieza; the Hero Saga runs from Goku and Piccolo to Super Saiyan Goku
- Double Sunday has its own sprite: two thinner yellow beams with glowing orb heads, fired side by side
- Saga messages are simpler (just where the bosses are), ready to be tied to quests later

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/8/2026 – Sagas[/b][/color]
- Menu > Sagas: start the Villain Saga (Raditz, Vegeta and Nappa, the Ginyu Force, Frieza, Cyborg Frieza and King Cold, Cooler, the Androids, Cell, Brolly, Babidi and Dabura, Majin Buu, Baby, Super 17, Shenron) or the Hero Saga (Goku and Piccolo through Super Saiyan 4 Gogeta)
- Pick a difficulty: the original Pathetic, Easy, Medium, Hard and Extreme (bosses as strong as the original's level-based stats), or Match my power
- Saga bosses land on Earth together; beat every boss in a part for the next one. Frieza, Cooler, Cell, Brolly, Majin Buu, Baby, Shenron and SSJ Gohan come back in their next form
- New saga AI (the original's was switched off because it was bugged): bosses chase and punch, power up when they're low, and use their own techniques: Raditz's Double Sunday, Nappa's Break Cannon and Giant Storm, Vegeta's Galick Gun, Guldo's Time Freeze, Burter's Space Mach Attack, Frieza's Death Beam and Death Ball, Android 19 and Dr. Gero's energy drain, Cell's Kamehameha and Solar Flare, Brolly's Eraser Cannon and Omega Blaster, Majin Buu's Candy Ray (turns you into chocolate), Baby's Revenge Death Ball, Omega Shenron's Minus Energy Power Ball, Krillin's Destructo Disc, Tien's Tri-Beam, Yamcha's Wolf Fang Fist, Gotenks' Super Ghost Kamikaze Attack, Gogeta's Big Bang Kamehameha and more
- Sagas are saved with your game and pick up where you left off
- You can no longer fly in Dr. Gero's Lab or on Beerus' planet, like the original

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/8/2026 – Monster AI[/b][/color]
- Monsters behave like the original: they stand still until you come within 12 tiles, then chase you quickly and attack about once a second; more than 13 tiles away they lose you and stay where they are (no more wandering home and healing)
- Monster attacks use the original formula, including their own critical hits (double damage); your dodge, block and counter work as before, and countering hits back for 130%
- Peaceful monsters only fight back once you attack them
- Killed monsters come back together every 2.5 minutes, like the original's respawner
- Dr. Gero's Lab: Exploding Saibamen and Exploding Cells glow red when they get close, then blow up, taking a big chunk of your powerlevel if you're caught in the blast (Saibamen 2-3 tiles, Cells 2-4); killing one first gives a full level, a chance of a boost to every stat and a chance to restore 10% of your powerlevel and Ki
- The Exploding Saibaman Boss keeps its distance and sends up to 15 exploding pawns at you
- Beerus' planet: its monsters hit for a percentage of your max powerlevel (less the higher your defence multiplier) every 2.6 seconds

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/8/2026 – Update notes, wave sizes[/b][/color]
- Added Update Notes and Legacy Update Notes (the original game's update log) to the Menu
- Explosive Wave is smaller: its two fire rings reach 1 and then 2 tiles, and it damages everything within 2 tiles
- Super Explosion Wave's three pulses are one tile smaller each (1, 2 and 3 tiles)
- Menu rearranged so everything fits on screen

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/8/2026 – Sound[/b][/color]
- Sound effects: punch and kick hits, getting hit, ki blasts, Kamehameha, Special Beam Cannon, discs, beams, Big Bang, explosions, teleporting, powering up, regenerating and critical hits
- Aura plays the original "aura full" sound
- Menu > Sound On / Off

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/8/2026 – Transformations[/b][/color]
- Transform opens "Which form would you like to transform to?" with every form you qualify for, like the original
- Transformation level: goes up when you transform and while you stay transformed. At level 25, Saiyans, Half Saiyans and Mystic Humans get Form 3 instead of Form 2, and top forms grow stronger with it
- Transformation speed: transforming takes 5.1 seconds at first and gets faster the more you do it
- Transforming has lightning and an explosion ring; each race gets its own Big Aura (Menu > Transformation Aura On / Off)
- Namekians, Majins, Androids, Dragons, Bio Androids, Tuffles and the other step races go up one form per press and their forms stack
- Androids, Bio Androids, Hitmen and Zealots can't revert, and being knocked out doesn't undo it
- Staying transformed no longer tires you out or forces you to revert (the original never did)
- Fixed Prideful Saiyan form levels; fixed Hitman, Bio Android, Dragon, Namekian, Saibaman Godly and Zealot forms; Fomhoire and Roshi forms use their real names
- Stats screen shows transformation level, speed and every form's requirements

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/8/2026 – Melee techniques[/b][/color]
- Push hits the enemy in front and throws it 1-3 tiles
- Power Dash charges up to 20 tiles leaving after-images and slams into the first enemy
- Burst Rush blinks to three sides of an enemy and throws it, leaving it stunned

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/8/2026 – Techniques match the original[/b][/color]
- Kamehameha and every blast use the original damage: (powerlevel x a + ki x b) x Ki Power%, minus the target's ki defence; critical hits (+35%) at Ki Cast/4 % chance
- Splash damage: blasts explode over a square (level 1 = 3x3), also when they hit a wall or run out of range
- Triple Ki Blasts fire three at once: in front and on both sides
- Rapid Blast, Ki Shower, Flame Shower, Shine Missile, Hell Zone Grenade, Acid Rain and Genocide fire homing ki missiles; Trace Nuke homes in too
- Big Bang Attack, Burning Attack, Blaster Shell and Revenge Death Ball are big 3x3 balls
- Explosive Wave hits everything around you for half your powerlevel with a spreading ring of fire; Super Explosion Wave pulses out three times; Final Explosion hits everything within 8 tiles and knocks you out
- Technique Ki costs match the original
- NPC teachers: SSJ4 Goku (X10 Kamehameha), SSJ3 Goku (Dragon Fist), Goku (After Image), Vegeta (Galick Gun, Final Flash, Big Bang, Final Shine), Piccolo (Zanzoken, Light Grenade, Special Beam Cannon), Tien (Solar Flare, Split Form, Tri Beam), Trunks (Finish Buster, Burning Attack), Yardrat Sensei, Frieza and Dr. Gero
- Side buttons are fixed to Rest, Talk, Fly, Power Up, Power Down, Transform and Revert, each shown only once you have it

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/8/2026 – Beams and Instant Transmission[/b][/color]
- Beams start on the tile in front of you instead of on top of you
- Beams use their real trail sprites instead of repeating the head, and the trail disappears with the head
- Diagonal beams are drawn as one straight line
- Instant Transmission works like the original: pick an NPC, 500 Ki, a few seconds to lock on, then appear next to them
- Removed Teleport from the side buttons

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/7/2026 – Test menu and minimap[/b][/color]
- Test button: level up 1-5,000 times, teleport to any NPC, enemy or coordinates, learn every technique, full restore
- Minimap: tap to switch between three sizes, drag to move it, remembers where you put it (Menu > Minimap Reset)
- Fixed menus that open another menu leaving an invisible copy behind

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/7/2026 – DBZ look[/b][/color]

- Side buttons are dragon balls with their names underneath
- Train button: Self Train, Meditate, Rest and Training Focus

[color=#ffd75e][b]11/7/2026 – Message window, minimap, stats[/b][/color]

- Minimap

- Joystick is easier to see

- Race and gene looks, hair and skin from the original icons
